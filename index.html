Privacy Policy

FV_Reel_publisher is a private tool used by Michael Florian (Off-Plate) to publish his own videos to his own Instagram accounts: @florivysvetluje, @florierklaert, @floriexplique and @floriexplica. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the tool uses

The tool uses Instagram's API only for the accounts above: it uploads videos, cover images and captions, reads basic account information (username, number of posts), reads view statistics of its own posts, and reads and replies to comments on its own posts.

What is stored

Nothing about other people is stored. Comments may be read to reply to them, but they are not saved, sold or shared. Access tokens for the accounts above are kept only on the owner's computer.
